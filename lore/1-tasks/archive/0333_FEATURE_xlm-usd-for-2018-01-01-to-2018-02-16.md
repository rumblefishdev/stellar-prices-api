# XLM/USD for 2018-01-01 → 2018-02-16

## Summary

[[0331]] prices pre-2021 candles from an hourly XLM/USD series loaded into
`prices.usd_rate`. That series starts at 2018-02-16 23:00 UTC. The priced
history should start at the beginning of 2018, so this task fills the 47 days
before it from the same exchange, in the same shape. The exchange had no
XLM/USD market in that period, so the rate is its XLM/BTC × BTC/USD.

## Context

- **Why the 0331 series stops at 2018-02-16.** The exchange delisted XLM/USD
  and XLM/EUR on 2017-08-22 and relisted them on 2018-02-16. Its trade ids run
  without a gap across that date: XLM/USD 225906 is at 2017-08-22 06:05 and
  225907 at 2018-02-16 23:43; XLM/EUR does the same. There was no trade to
  fetch, so the OHLCVT files would be empty for the range too. Measured
  2026-10-16. Supersedes the 2026-10-14 reading that the trades API could not
  reach older trades.
- **XLM/BTC and BTC/USD traded throughout** on the same exchange and page from
  its public trades API: 472,990 XLM/BTC and ~2.3 M BTC/USD trades for
  2017-12-31 → 2018-02-28. XLM/BTC has a trade in 1,074 of the range's 1,127
  hours, median 296 trades an hour.
- **The exchange was down 2018-01-11 07:00 → 01-13 14:00** (53 hours without
  an XLM/BTC trade, in four runs). A second exchange's XLM/BTC × BTC/USDT
  covers them, but mixes sources and takes USDT at $1, so it is not used.
- **What is left to price** (prod, 2026-10-15): 6,432 1h rows with volume
  quoted in XLM or a dollar token, worth 119.2 M XLM of quote volume, all at
  `close_usd = 0`.
- **Out of scope:** everything before 2018-01-01. There are 12,819 such 1h
  rows (2015-11 → 2017-12), and they stay unpriced.

## Measurements (2026-10-16, hourly VWAP of each leg)

| Cross rate compared with | Hourly median / p95 / max | Daily median / max |
|---|---|---|
| The exchange's direct XLM/USD (the 0331 definition), 2018-02-17 12:00 → 02-28 | 0.44 % / 1.87 % / 6.40 % | 0.21 % / 0.64 % |
| Second exchange's XLM/BTC × the exchange's BTC/USD, in range | 0.41 % / 4.19 % / 21.0 % | 0.33 % / 5.87 % |
| Second exchange's XLM/BTC × BTC/USDT (lab cross-check), in range | 0.98 % / 4.67 % / 20.3 % | 0.95 % / 8.06 % |

The direct pair's first hours after relisting were off the market: at
2018-02-16 23:00 it printed 0.4056 against 0.4505 for the cross rate (11.1 %),
and 3–4 % for the next six hours. The 23:00 hour is already in the 0331
series as `external`.

## Decision (amended 2026-10-16, Adam)

Supersedes the 2026-10-15 source decision.

- **Range:** 2018-01-01 00:00 → 2018-02-16 22:00 UTC.
- **Source:** the same exchange, as a cross rate: hourly VWAP of XLM/BTC ×
  hourly VWAP of BTC/USD, both from its public trades API, stamped at the hour
  start. An hour gets a rate only when both legs traded in it.
- **The outage hours stay without a rate.** The pass carries a rate for up to
  24 h, so only the candles more than 24 h past the last rate stay unpriced.
  XLM's daily candles are priced on every day of the range.
- **The 0331 row at 2018-02-16 23:00 is replaced** with the cross rate.
- **Tag:** `reference_asset = 'third-party'`, as for 0331.

## Implementation Plan

1. Build the hourly series for the range plus the 2018-02-16 23:00 hour.
2. Insert as `('native','XLM','','')`, staged `external-candidate`, then
   promote; the 23:00 row by a newer version.
3. Re-run 0331's `coarse-repair --pre-usdc-xlm-rate` for 201801–201802, first
   1h, then the wider tiers.
4. Count the candles left unpriced around the outage.
