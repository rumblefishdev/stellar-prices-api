# XLM/USD for 2017-01-17 → 2018-02-16

## Summary

[[0331]] prices pre-2021 candles from an hourly XLM/USD series loaded into
`prices.usd_rate`. That series starts at 2018-02-16 23:00 UTC, so XLM- and
dollar-quoted candles before it stay at `close_usd = 0`. This task extends the
series back to 2017-01-17, when the same exchange opened its XLM/USD market,
and loads it in the same shape. That prices almost all of the history before
2018-02-16.

## Context

- **Why the 0331 series stops at 2018-02-16.** The exchange's trades API
  cannot reach trades before 2018-02-16 23:03 when paged. A time-based `since`
  anywhere from 2017-12 to 2018-02-16 lands on that first reachable trade, and
  paging from the start jumps to it after the first 1,000 trades. Probed
  2026-10-14.
- **When the exchange's markets start** (public trades API, `since=0`, probed
  2026-10-15): XLM/USD on 2017-01-17 20:17 UTC (first price $0.00225), and
  XLM/BTC on 2016-07-19.
- **What is left to price** (prod, 1h rows with volume quoted in XLM or a
  dollar token, all at `close_usd = 0`, 2026-10-15):

  | Period | 1h rows | Quote volume (M XLM) |
  |---|---|---|
  | 2015-11-18 → 2016-07-19 | 32 | 0.6 |
  | 2016-07-19 → 2017-01-17 | 36 | 0.2 |
  | 2017-01-17 → 2017-12-31 | 12,751 | 383.6 |
  | 2018-01-01 → 2018-02-16 | 6,432 | 119.2 |

  XLM/USD from 2017-01-17 covers 19,183 of these 19,251 rows (99.6 %).
- **Thin market early on.** The first three XLM/USD trades are spread over
  three days. 0331's rate window on tiers ≤ 1h is 24 h, so a candle with no
  trade on the exchange in the preceding 24 h stays unpriced.

## Decision (2026-10-15, Adam)

- **Source:** the exchange's downloadable historical OHLCVT files, which
  cover each pair "from the beginning of each market". This is option 1 of
  the original task. They have to be fetched by hand.
- **Range:** 2017-01-17 → 2018-02-16 22:00 UTC.
- **Left unpriced:** the 68 in-scope 1h rows before 2017-01-17. Pricing them
  would need XLM/BTC × BTC/USD or another exchange, which is not worth it for
  that volume.

## Implementation Plan

1. Fetch the OHLCVT archive and record its provenance (file, sha256, date)
   and the terms of use.
2. Build the hourly XLM/USD series for 2017-01-17 → 2018-02-16 22:00 UTC from
   the 60-minute XLM/USD file, using the same rate definition as the 0331 rows.
3. Check the seam: compare it with the 0331 series where both have a value
   (from 2018-02-16 23:00), and compare the 0331 range itself against the file.
4. Insert in the same shape as 0331's rows (`('native','XLM','','')`, staged
   `external-candidate` first, same verify queries), then promote together with
   them or on top of them.
5. Re-run 0331's `coarse-repair --pre-usdc-xlm-rate` for 201701–201802: 1h
   first, then the wider tiers, which 0331 builds from 1h.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Source recorded with provenance and terms of use.
- [ ] Hourly XLM/USD rows for every hour of 2017-01-17 → 2018-02-16 that has a
      trade on the exchange.
- [ ] Agreement with the 0331 series on the overlap recorded (median and max
      deviation).
- [ ] XLM 1d candles from 2017-01-18 on return a USD price wherever the
      exchange traded within the window; the remaining days counted and
      explained, including the 68 rows before 2017-01-17.
