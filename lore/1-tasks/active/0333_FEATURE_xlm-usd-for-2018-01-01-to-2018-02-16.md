# XLM/USD for 2018-01-01 → 2018-02-16

## Summary

[[0331]] prices pre-2021 candles from an hourly XLM/USD series loaded into
`prices.usd_rate`. That series starts at 2018-02-16 23:00 UTC. The priced
history should start at the beginning of 2018, so this task fills the 47 days
before it, from the same exchange, in the same shape.

## Context

- **Why the 0331 series stops at 2018-02-16.** The exchange's trades API
  cannot reach trades before 2018-02-16 23:03 when paged. A time-based `since`
  anywhere from 2017-12 to 2018-02-16 lands on that first reachable trade, and
  paging from the start jumps to it after the first 1,000 trades. Probed
  2026-10-14.
- **The market did trade then.** Its XLM/USD market opened on 2017-01-17 and
  had about 1,000 trades a day around 2018-01-01.
- **What is left to price** (prod, 2026-10-15): 6,432 1h rows with volume
  quoted in XLM or a dollar token, worth 119.2 M XLM of quote volume, all at
  `close_usd = 0`.
- **Out of scope:** everything before 2018-01-01. There are 12,819 such 1h
  rows (2015-11 → 2017-12), and they stay unpriced.
- **A cross-check is available.** A second exchange's public hourly XLM/BTC ×
  BTC/USDT tracks the 0331 series at a median deviation of 0.43 % per day
  (0.66 % for 2018-02-17 → 04-30). This was measured on 2026-10-15 for the
  synthetic XLM series lab.

## Decision (2026-10-15, Adam)

- **Range:** 2018-01-01 00:00 → 2018-02-16 22:00 UTC.
- **Source:** the exchange's downloadable historical OHLCVT files. They cover
  each pair from the start of its market and have to be fetched by hand. Same
  source as the 0331 series.

## Implementation Plan

1. Fetch the OHLCVT archive and record its provenance (file, sha256, date)
   and its terms of use.
2. Build the hourly XLM/USD series for the range from the 60-minute XLM/USD
   file, using the same rate definition as the 0331 rows (hourly VWAP stamped
   at the hour start).
3. Check the seam against the 0331 series where both have a value (from
   2018-02-16 23:00), and the range against the XLM/BTC × BTC/USDT
   cross-check.
4. Insert in the same shape as the 0331 rows: `('native','XLM','','')`,
   `reference_asset = 'third-party'`, staged `external-candidate` first, then
   promote.
5. Re-run 0331's `coarse-repair --pre-usdc-xlm-rate` for 201801–201802, first
   1h, then the wider tiers.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Source recorded with provenance and terms of use.
- [ ] Hourly XLM/USD rows for every hour of 2018-01-01 → 2018-02-16 that has a
      trade on the exchange.
- [ ] Agreement recorded with the 0331 series on the overlap and with the
      cross-check (median and max deviation).
- [ ] XLM daily USD prices from 2018-01-01 on, wherever the exchange traded
      within the window; any remaining days counted and explained.
